## 🌟 Overview

Roblox Friend Manager is a Chrome-compatible browser extension that gives you a clean, intuitive dashboard to review, organize, and manage your Roblox friend list. Instead of clicking through profile after profile, you get a single view where your friends are laid out, searchable, and sortable — with batch actions that let you handle dozens of entries in seconds.

The goal is simple: make friend-list management feel less like a chore and more like tidying a room. You decide what stays, what goes, and how your social circle looks. No confusion, no clutter, no wasted afternoons.

This project is built for players who value their time and want a tool that respects their attention. It works quietly in the background, opens only when you need it, and never gets in the way of your gameplay.

## ⚙️ How It Works

At a high level, the extension injects a management layer over your existing Roblox friend page. It reads the friend entries already rendered by the site, presents them in a friendlier dashboard, and routes your chosen actions back through the standard UI interactions. This means the tool behaves like an accelerated set of hands rather than a mysterious background process.

A typical session looks like this:

1. Open your Roblox friends page as usual.
2. Activate the extension from the toolbar.
3. The dashboard slides in, populated with your current friend list.
4. Use search, filters, and tags to narrow down who you want to manage.
5. Select entries and choose an action — the extension performs the steps you would have done manually, but in an organized batch.
6. Review the summary and, if needed, undo recent changes within the grace window.

Because the extension follows the same flow a human would, it stays compatible with the site's expectations and reduces the chance of unexpected behavior.

## 🚀 Performance and Reliability

Performance is measured in milliseconds saved and frustrations avoided. The extension is engineered to:

- Load in a fraction of a second, even on modest hardware.
- Use event delegation rather than heavy polling.
- Cache list data locally for the duration of a session so repeated actions stay snappy.
- Fail gracefully — if something unexpected happens, the tool reports it clearly instead of silently breaking.

Reliability also means predictability. Actions are queued and paced to avoid hammering the site with rapid-fire requests, which keeps your account's activity looking natural and calm.

## 🛡️ Safety and Responsible Usage

Using any tool that interacts with an online account deserves care. Roblox Friend Manager is designed with responsible usage in mind:

- **No credential collection** — the extension never asks for your password.
- **No background automation** — nothing happens unless you explicitly click.
- **Local-only processing** — list data stays in your browser session.
- **Paced actions** — changes are spread out to avoid unusual activity spikes.

Always review your selections before confirming bulk actions. The undo window exists as a safety net, but intentional review is the best safeguard of all.
